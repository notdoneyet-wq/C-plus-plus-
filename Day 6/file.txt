# Pre/Post increment operators


1) Post : x++, x-- [First assign then use the variable]
2) Pre : --x, ++x  [First use the variable in the assingment then assign]

i) if its given y = ++ x , x=5

here two things are happening, one is increment and the other is assingment

first we increment x , then we assign the resulted value to y.

i.e : y = 6 , x = 6.

ii) if its given y = x ++ , x = 5

first we assign y = x and then increment the value of x.

ie. : y = 5 , x = 6.

eg 1: z = x ++ * y , where x = 5 , y = 10 .

here, y = 10, z = 50, x = 6.

eg 2: z = ++ x * y , where x = 5 , y = 10 .

here , y = 10 , z = 60 , x = 6.
